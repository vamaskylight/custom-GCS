# VAMA GCS custom build

This folder is our QGroundControl custom build.
It started from QGC's `custom-example` (v5.1.5) and was changed for VAMA:

- ArduPilot only. QGC's own ArduPilot support is on, PX4 is off.
- App name "VAMA GCS" (build name `VAMA-GCS`), Android package `com.vama.gcs`.
- Icons and logo come from `vgcs/assets/Vama Logo.png`. Regenerate them with `python apk/make_icons.py`.
- The example's PX4 plugins, PerimeterScan mission item, demo button and custom instrument panel were removed, so the normal QGC fly view shows.

VAMA additions:

- `src/Skydroid/`: the Skydroid camera link (protocol, UDP and TCP link, laser target maths). See the comment at the top of `SkydroidLink.h` for what the field taught.
- `src/Skydroid/ViewproProtocol.*`, `ViewproCamera.*`, `SiyiProtocol.*`, `SiyiCamera.*`: the Viewpro (A20KTR) and SIYI (ZR10) cameras for the app (task AK). Plain C++ ports of VGCS's protocols and camera rules (no sockets, no timers: a command gives the frames to send, `tick()` the frames due later). Checked against VGCS's Python code: `test/gen_siyi_vectors.py` and `test/gen_viewpro_vectors.py` write the bytes, `test/gen_siyi_camera_vectors.py` and `test/gen_viewpro_camera_vectors.py` replay action scripts through VGCS's adapters with a fake clock. Host tests in `run_tests.ps1`. Since 2026-10-26 the app's camera link uses them (see the cameras below).
- `src/Skydroid/LaserGeo.*`: where a point is. By laser (the range along the camera's look), and without the laser (the look followed down to level ground, by VGCS's rules: "Tap on the video: from the picture" in the camera settings, and the fallback when the laser gives no reading). Both are ports of `vgcs/observe/geo_reference.py`; `test/gen_laser_vectors.py` writes the cases the host test compares them with.
- `src/Skydroid/ObjectTracker.*`: the object lock's own tracker (plain C++, no Qt). The app finds the locked object in pictures of the video and turns the camera with speed commands, because the camera's own tracker did not follow on the V13 (field videos of 2026-10-06 and 2026-10-09). It learns the object's colours and its layout, so a person who turns and walks is kept. `SkydroidLink::lockPicture` takes the pictures, `FlightDisplayViewVideoOutput.qml` hands them over about 14 times a second. Tests: `test/object_tracker_test.cc` (stiff objects, grey pictures) and `test/object_tracker_walk_test.cc` (a walking, turning figure in colour and thermal).
- `src/Skydroid/LockCheck.*`: the object finder's check of a lock on a person or a vehicle (plain C++, no Qt, task AC). The finder looks about once a second (`ObjectMarks` "checking"). Not on the box in 3 looks in a row: NOT SEEN, the camera waits. Not seen, and one of its kind near where it went: the lock goes on there. A lock waits 8 s for its object before it ends. Objects under 48 of 720 points are not checked. `SkydroidLink::finderLooked` acts on it. Tests: `test/lock_check_test.cc` (part of `run_tests.ps1`), the check tests in `test/link/skydroid_link_test.cc`, and `run_preview.ps1`.
- `src/Skydroid/FollowGuide.*`, `ObjectFollow.*`, `FollowDrone.h`, `VehicleFollowDrone.*`: Follow Me. The drone flies after the object of the app's lock, and keeps the distance, the height and the heading it had at the start. Never flown on a real drone yet.
  - `SkydroidLink` says where the locked object is on the ground, for every picture it is found in (`objectPlaced`): the camera's angle to it and the drone's height, over level ground. A laser range on the object corrects that where the ground there is higher than the take-off point.
  - `FollowGuide` (plain C++, no Qt) holds every rule, and gives a speed over the ground five times a second. The rules are listed at the top of `FollowGuide.h`: it starts 20 m or more from the object and 10 m up or more, never flies the drone nearer than 15 m, never changes the height or the heading, has a speed limit and a range limit, and stops when the lock is lost, when the pilot changes the flight mode, or when the drone does not fly what it is asked.
  - `ObjectFollow` (QML singleton) runs it for the screen: the follow button beside the lock button, the window that asks first, and the state in plain words.
  - `VehicleFollowDrone` is the drone: telemetry from the autopilot's own MAVLink messages, and the speed sent in Guided mode (`SET_POSITION_TARGET_LOCAL_NED` with the heading to keep, see `kSpeedTypeMask`). Only an ArduPilot multicopter is flown.
  - Tests: `test/follow_guide_test.cc` (a bench with a made-up object and a drone that answers as ArduCopter does; part of `run_tests.ps1`), `test/follow_sitl_test.py` (the same guide flying ArduCopter 4.6.2 and 4.7.0 in their simulator: `py apk/custom/test/follow_sitl_test.py`, about 20 minutes, set-up in `drone/README.md`), the place and Follow tests in `test/link/skydroid_link_test.cc` (with a stand-in drone), and `test/link/follow_drone_test.cc` (the MAVLink side, with the real MAVLink headers of the last APK build). `run_preview.ps1` tries the button and the window.
- `src/Skydroid/ObjectFinder*`, `MarksWorker.*`, `ObjectMarks*`: marks on people and vehicles. While the lock button waits for an object, the app marks the people and vehicles it finds in the video, and a tap on a mark locks that object. Never run on the remote control yet.
  - `ObjectFinder` (plain C++, no Qt) is the finder: a small neural network, NanoDet-Plus-m, run by the ncnn library. `ObjectFinderNcnn.cc` is the only file that includes ncnn. What is made of the network's answer, and what it is given to look at, is in `ObjectFinder.cc`. `ObjectFinderNone.cc` takes the place of `ObjectFinderNcnn.cc` in a build without the library.
  - The network's two files and the licence notices are in `res/ai` (`MODEL_SOURCE.md` says where they come from, with their hashes). The library is not in the repo: `apk/build_ncnn.ps1` builds it from its source.
  - `MarksWorker` looks at a picture on a thread of its own: all of it made smaller, and its middle at its own size, side by side. How large that middle is depends on how fast the device is (`planLooks` in `ObjectFinder.h`).
  - `ObjectMarks` (QML singleton) asks for pictures while the lock button waits, keeps the marks, and takes the tap. Each mark carries how sure the finder is. Under `kSureScore` (50 %) it is shown thinner and faint with a "?" ("Vehicle 46%?"), and the state line counts it as unsure (task AD: a higher score limit took away real people about as fast as wrong marks, measured on 1169 photo views). Marks under `scoreMin` (40 %, `ObjectFinder.h`) are not shown.
  - `SkydroidLink::lockAtFound` starts the lock on the picture a mark was found in, and goes through the pictures since (the link keeps the last 2.5 seconds of pictures while marks show). So the lock is on the object where it is now, also when it moved on since the mark was drawn.
  - `FlightDisplayViewVideoOutput.qml` hands over the finder's pictures (1280 points wide, whenever `ObjectMarks.wantsPicture()` says so). `FlyViewCustomLayer.qml` draws the marks, the corners of the part looked at closely and the state line, and has the switch and the licences in the camera settings.
  - Tests: `test/object_finder_test.cc` (the arithmetic, part of `run_tests.ps1`), `test/link/marks_net_test.cc` (the real network and library on this PC, with three photos from the air in `test/ai_pictures`), the marks tests in `test/link/skydroid_link_test.cc` (with a stand-in for the network), `run_preview.ps1` (the screen), and `test/run_android_link_check.ps1` (the finder and the library compile and link for both Android ABIs, without an APK build).
  - What it finds and what it does not, as measured: phase 8 of `DOCS/APK-V1-DEV-PLAN.md`.
- `src/FlyViewCustomLayer.qml`: the camera screen (replaces QGC's empty file of the same name). Icons are `res/Images/vama_*.svg`.
  - Photos and recording (task AL, client 2026-10-26: "photos and recordings are not saved in rc"): the buttons tell the camera to take a photo (CAP) or to start or stop recording (REC 01, REC 00), and the camera saves them on its own SD card. Until test build 13 every record press sent REC 01, so the camera never stopped. `SkydroidLink` asks the camera whether it records (every 2 s) and for its SD card's room (SDC), and after each press `cameraNote` says what the camera did: a note at the top, under the other boxes there. The status shows "REC?" until the camera says that it records, and "No SD card" when it has none. VGCS sends the same frames (`vgcs/skydroid/protocol.py`, `build_record`).
  - A copy on the RC as well (the client's Stage 1.1): the photo button also asks QGC's `VideoManager` for a picture of the video (`grabImage`, QGC's Photo folder), and the record button also records the video the RC receives (`startRecording`, `stopRecording`, QGC's Video folder, mp4). So photo and record are there for every camera. The record button shows a recording while the camera or the RC records, and one press decides for both (`SkydroidLink::setRecord`). The status shows "RC REC" while the RC records. QGC does not say when a picture is written, so the note says only where it goes. Tests: the vectors of `test/skydroid_top_test.cc`, the photo and record tests of `test/link/skydroid_link_test.cc`, and screenshots 35 to 37 of `run_preview.ps1` (its stand-in for QGC's `VideoManager` records and grabs).
  - The cameras (task AA, client 2026-10-16): what the app knows of a camera is a profile (`src/Skydroid/CameraProfile.h`): its addresses, lens, zoom kind, thermal lens, how far it turns, laser, photo, recording and tracker of its own. The V12, V13 and V14 Pro are built in with the numbers the app was tested with, and cannot be changed. In Advanced Mode the camera settings have a "Cameras..." button: `src/VamaCameraEditor.qml` copies a camera into a new one, changes and deletes added ones. An added camera is a file in the app's folder `Cameras` (`Android/data/com.vama.gcs/files/Cameras`), so it can be copied to another remote control by USB. Choosing a camera also sets its addresses and QGC's video; the screen shows only the buttons that camera can use. A camera's file names its protocol (`"protocol"`: `"skydroid-top"`, `"viewpro"` or `"siyi"`); another protocol needs code. The Viewpro A20KTR and the SIYI ZR10 are built in (task AK): `SkydroidLink` then sends no TOP frame at all (`_sendTo`), but the frames of `ViewproCamera` (over one TCP connection, port 2000) or `SiyiCamera` (by UDP, port 37260) at each place where it sends TOP (gimbal speed and stop, angles in one frame for both axes, centre, point down, zoom, photo, recording), and a timer sends what those rules have due later (a zoom click's stop, a Viewpro's hold after a jog). A Viewpro says its angles, zoom, view and recording in its status, asked twice a second; a SIYI says its angles only, its zoom (`"pulse"`) is not known until "back to 1x" has zoomed out for 8 s, and until then a point from the picture is placed at the cross only. A Viewpro's thermal picture comes in its day video after a sensor switch (`"thermalBySwitch": true`): the IR button then calls `switchThermal` instead of switching QGC's video address. Viewpro and SIYI yaw count to the right, as VGCS takes them. A VPro's laser ranges as VGCS ranges with it (B13 c): continuous ranging, the first range the camera measured after the shot, and off 10 s after the last shot, when the link turns off or another camera is chosen. The app uses no laser of a SIYI and no tracker of a Viewpro or SIYI camera (the editor hides those boxes). The V20 (Skydroid C20, task AJ) is built in too: it takes TOP over TCP port 5000 only, so its file says `"transport": "tcp"` (the editor's "Control link"). `SkydroidLink` then keeps one TCP connection to the camera's own address (no other addresses or ports), cuts the frames out of the byte stream (`top::takeFrames`, a port of VGCS's `split_top_frames`) and opens the connection again 2 s after it is lost or 3 s after a try that does not connect. VGCS reads and writes the same files (`vgcs/skydroid/camera_files.py`, with the same editor), so a Skydroid camera's file copied between the two adds the camera there too; a Viewpro or SIYI file is read by VGCS and named in its notes as for the app only (VGCS sets those cameras up in its own settings). Tests: `test/link/camera_profile_test.cc` (the built-in cameras against the link's old formulas, the files, and VGCS's files: `test/gen_camera_files.py` writes them into `test/camera_files/vgcs`, and the app's own files of the same cameras are kept in `test/camera_files/apk` for VGCS's test), the camera tests of `skydroid_link_test.cc`, and the camera steps of `run_preview.ps1`.
- `src/FlyViewTopRightColumnLayout.qml`: replaces QGC's file to remove QGC's own photo and video buttons, which send MAVLink camera commands the Skydroid camera does not use.
- `src/SelectViewDropdown.qml`: QGC's app menu with the VAMA mark on the Settings button and VAMA version text. QGC tints menu icons through its `coloredsvg` image provider, which reads `/res/QGCLogoWhite.svg` directly, so only a copy of this file can change that icon. Change the "Test build" line for every APK sent out.
  - Advanced Mode (with Parameters) asks for the admin password (task Z, client 2026-10-16): the hidden tap on the version line shows `src/VamaAdminPasswordPanel.qml` in the menu, in place of its buttons. It is not a window of its own, because QGC's drawer destroys the menu when it closes, and a window made from the menu would then stop working. `src/AdminPassword.cc` checks the password (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, a salted hash only) and makes the next try wait after a wrong one. `CustomPlugin::unlockAdvanced` turns Advanced Mode on, and `CustomPlugin::_advancedChanged` turns it off again when anything else turns it on. Test builds have the test password `vama1234`. Customer builds have the team's, from `apk\make_admin_password.ps1` (see `apk/README.md`). VGCS's camera editor asks for the same password (`vgcs/app/admin_password.py`), and a customer exe gets the team's from the same file (`packaging\build_exe.py --customer`). Tests: `test/link/admin_password_test.cc` (built twice, as a test build and as a customer build) and the menu steps of `run_preview.ps1` (`test/preview/menu.qml`).
  - "Camera settings", and in Advanced Mode "Cameras" (task AG, client 2026-10-21): the client looked for the camera windows in the menu. The menu only asks for them (`CustomPlugin::requestCameraWindow`, signal `cameraWindowRequested`), closes and shows the fly view. `FlyViewCustomLayer.qml` opens the window once the menu is gone: the camera settings from their top, and the editor only in Advanced Mode. Tests: the menu steps of `run_preview.ps1`.
- QGC's other logos are replaced by files with the same name under `/Custom/res` in `custom.qrc` (`QGCLogoFull.svg`, and `QGCLogoArrow.svg` for the phone position on the map). All logo files come from `apk/make_icons.py`.
- `src/AppSettings.qml`: a copy of QGC's Application Settings screen (client feedback 2026-10-07). The General page shows the VAMA mark (QGC tints it through `coloredsvg` too), and the Help page with QGC's links is left out.
- `src/FlightDisplayViewVideoOutput.qml`: a copy of QGC's video picture with the thermal colour modes of VGCS (client request 2026-10-07). While IR shows the thermal stream, the picture is drawn through a shader (`res/shaders/thermal_palette.frag`) that looks up each grey level in `res/Images/thermal_palettes.png`. The table, the compiled shader (`.qsb`, so the build needs no shader tools) and the test data come from `python apk/make_thermal_palettes.py`, which reads VGCS's own colour tables. The colour button beside IR and its list are in `src/FlyViewCustomLayer.qml`, the chosen mode in `SkydroidLink` (saved). The app's own digital zoom (B10, `SkydroidLink.digitalZoom`, 1x, 2x and 4x by the "D" button under the zoom buttons) scales this item about its centre: QGC's video view clips it, and `_videoRect()` in `src/FlyViewCustomLayer.qml` grows with it, so taps, the lock box and the marks stay places in the whole picture. The grabs are of the item without its scale, so the lock and the marks get the whole picture.
- `src/VehicleWarnings.qml`: a copy of QGC's "No GPS Lock for Vehicle" and pre-arm texts. With the video as the main view they show small, in the lower part of the picture: QGC's large texts lay over the object to lock (client video, 2026-10-09). With the map as the main view nothing changes.
- `src/ProximityRadarValues.qml` and `src/ProximityStore.*`: the obstacle radar on the map and on the video (A7, the client's LiDAR and TF02 Pro, 2026-10-27). QGC's radar took the drone's DISTANCE_SENSOR facts, which keep each message by its direction only and never clear a value. With the client's RPLidar A3 (ArduPilot knows no range for it, so an empty direction comes as 0) that put a "0.00" label in the middle of the video for each empty direction, all on top of each other; the last arcs stayed for ever once the sensor stopped (ArduPilot then sends no direction); with a known range the empty directions were red arcs on the range circle; and the range circle took the range of the last message of any sensor, so a downward rangefinder beside the LiDAR changed its size. `ProximityStore` (a QML singleton, fed by `CustomPlugin::mavlinkMessage`) keeps one `ProximitySectors` for each drone from the proximity sensor's own messages only (ids 10 to 17), each with its own range: an empty direction, or one not heard for 2 s, has no value (NaN). The copy of QGC's `ProximityRadarValues.qml` reads it, so both of QGC's views leave such a direction out. A TF02 Pro looking ahead is set up as an obstacle sensor (PRX type 4, VGCS's obstacle settings page), and ArduPilot then sends it in these directions, not as a rangefinder. Tests: `test/link/proximity_store_test.cc` (in `run_link_test.ps1`) and `py test/proximity_view_test.py`, which renders QGC's own two views from `apk/qgc-src` with this copy and a stand-in for the store on Qt 6.11.1 (through PySide6, offscreen); `run_preview.ps1` does not show them.
- Every copy of a QGC file marks its changes "VAMA". Compare each copy with QGC's file again after moving to a new QGC release.
- `src/CustomPlugin.cc`: RC channel stream default 10 Hz (QGC asks for 2), because the RC wheel moves the camera. It also shows QGC's 3D view switch and its "3D View" settings page again (client request 2026-10-08): QGC 5.1.5 hides both on Android, because "rendering is not yet reliable across Android GPUs and drivers". The switch stays off by default, and "Keep 3D scene alive" is off by default here (QGC: on), because a remote control is short of memory. It is a test until it has run on the client's remote control. In the 3D view `FlyViewCustomLayer.qml` leaves taps and drags to the 3D view: the camera never turns and the laser never fires from there (the screen preview checks it).
- `src/Maps/`: the map packs that VGCS exports (task U, client 2026-10-15). `MapPack.*` reads a pack folder and writes it as a QGC tile file. `MapPackLoader.*` (started by `CustomPlugin::init`) loads new packs at the start through QGC's own import, only checks a pack loaded before, says in one message what was loaded and what not, and switches the map to the pack's. While a pack loads, the fly view says so at the top (`CustomPlugin.mapPackLoading`). After a pack was loaded, with no position from the drone, the fly view moves the map to the middle of the pack's area at zoom 15 (`CustomPlugin.mapPackLoaded`, task AB). Tests: `test/link/map_pack_test.cc` and `test/link/map_pack_loader_test.cc`, and the screen preview.

QML files replace QGC's through `custom.qrc` (prefix `/Custom/qml`, same path as QGC's file). They are not compiled at build time, so a mistake shows only when the app runs. Run `test/run_preview.ps1` after every QML change: it loads the real QML on this PC and prints every QML warning.

Still from the example, for later use: the `Custom.Widgets` QML module in `res/Custom/Widgets` and the colour palette in `src/CustomPlugin.cc`.

Build steps and rules are in `apk/README.md`.
