# Test pictures for the object finder

Three views from the air, each 1280 x 720 points (the camera's picture size).
`marks_net_test` (in `apk/custom/test/link`) runs the app's finder on them.
They are for the tests only. They do not go into the app.

Each view is a part of a photo from Wikimedia Commons: the photo scaled to 1920 points wide, then a part of 1280 x 720 points cut out and saved as JPEG.
The photos are under Creative Commons licences that ask for the author's name and, for changed copies, the same licence.
So each view here is under the licence of its photo.

## What they are for

- `bridge_walkers.jpg`: people walking on a footbridge, seen from a drone, 35 to 55 points tall, and a group further away at about 30 points.
- `car_park.jpg`: a car park from a drone: single cars, rows of cars close together, a few people.
- `sea_front.jpg`: people and cars at a building site by the sea. It also shows what the finder gets wrong: it takes two traffic cones for people, and a pallet of bricks for a vehicle.

The sizes above are in points of these views.
The camera of the drone shows a person 1.7 m tall about 30 points high at 50 m, with its widest lens (83 degrees across 1280 points).
